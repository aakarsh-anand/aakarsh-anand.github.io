\section{Research Experience}
\role{Graduate Student Researcher}{Sankararaman Lab, UCLA \note{(undergraduate researcher 2022--24)}}{Jan 2022 -- present}

\thread{Foundation models for health data}{}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Wearable foundation models.} Inertia-1 (NeurIPS 2026 main track, co-first author, with Yuzhe Yang's lab), an open study of
    pretraining on wrist accelerometry, with 5M--100M-parameter models in six architectures, 18.2M~hours from
    115K+~people, and 15 evaluation datasets. Worked on much of the pretraining and evaluation, and showed that
    combining sensors consistently beats any single sensor.
  \item \textbf{Disease prediction and genetics.} A follow-up journal paper (in prep., co-first author) applies the models
    to diagnosis, prognosis, and disease progression in five cohorts and studies the genetics of the learned traits, with
    early work pairing them with LLMs.
  \item \textbf{Genetics-aware training.} Developing a loss that pushes a model's learned traits to be heritable and genetically
    correlated with disease.
\end{itemize}

\thread{Statistical genetics at biobank scale}{}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Gene--gene interactions.} Co-developed scalable tests for interactions between variants (QuadKAST, co-first
    author, and FAME) and ran their calibration and power simulations. Now extending it to rare disease,
    supported by an Anthropic AI for Science grant.
  \item \textbf{Gene--environment interactions.} Helped develop ENGINE, which learns which combinations of exposures
    change genetic effects, and REGENA (second author, in revision), which separates trait scale and amplification from
    real differences in genetic effects.
  \item \textbf{Genetic effects across genes, cells, and tissues.} Ran real-data analyses for FLEX, which estimates the
    heritability of rare variants gene by gene. Also building a single-cell model of genetic effects across cell
    types and a genetic-correlation atlas across 53 tissues.
\end{itemize}
